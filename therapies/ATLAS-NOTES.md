# Therapy Atlas: editor notes

Notes from the research pass, for review. Entries marked *medium* show a short “check before relying on this” line on their page.

- **Metacognitive therapy (MCT)** (medium; evidence: moderate): Founding year approximate (early 1990s; S-REF model 1994, GAD model mid-1990s). Strength of independent evidence relative to CBT is debated.
- **Unified Protocol for Transdiagnostic Treatment of Emotional Disorders (UP)** (high; evidence: moderate): Family chosen (cb); source states it is not in the trauma family. Availability in UK NHS services is not well documented.
- **App-based and online CBT** (medium; evidence: moderate): Founding year is approximate (computerised CBT emerged gradually). The specific programmes covered by NICE HTE8/HTE9 change over time.
- **Trauma-focused cognitive behavioural therapy (TF-CBT)** (high; evidence: strong): Founding year approximate; 'TF-CBT' refers both to the Cohen-Mannarino-Deblinger child model and to the wider UK umbrella term, entry covers both.
- **Narrative exposure therapy (NET)** (high; evidence: strong): Exact founding year approximate (first trial c. 2004; manual 2005).
- **Skills Training in Affective and Interpersonal Regulation (STAIR)** (medium; evidence: moderate): NICE NG116 mentions phased approaches with stabilisation for complex PTSD but I did not confirm whether STAIR is named; entry says it is not specifically named. UK availability poorly documented.
- **Mentalisation-based treatment (MBT)** (medium; evidence: moderate): NICE BPD guidance (CG78) does not specifically recommend MBT, so NICE is not mentioned. Family chosen as pd; could also be considered integrative.
- **Psychodynamic psychotherapy** (medium; evidence: moderate): Founding year 1940 is a rough convention for the emergence of psychodynamic psychotherapy as distinct from psychoanalysis; there is no single founding date.
- **Object relations therapy** (high; evidence: limited): Year 1940 is approximate; the school developed over the 1930s to 1950s.
- **Lacanian psychoanalysis** (medium; evidence: limited): Unsure exactly which UK registers Lacanian organisations currently belong to, so this is stated cautiously.
- **Brief psychodynamic psychotherapy** (medium; evidence: strong): NICE NG222 lists short-term psychodynamic psychotherapy in its tables of treatment options for depression (less and more severe), but not as a first choice; exact wording not re-checked. Focal psychodynamic therapy for adult anorexia in NG69 recalled rather than freshly verified. DIT availability varies by NHS Talking Therapies service.
- **Existential therapy** (medium; evidence: limited): Year 1958 (Rollo May's edited book Existence) is a conventional marker; existential therapy has no single founding date.
- **Logotherapy** (medium; evidence: limited): Founding year uncertain: Frankl used the term in the 1920s or 1930s; 1938 is a commonly cited date for its first published use.
- **Emotion-focused therapy (EFT)** (medium; evidence: moderate): Year 2002 refers to the adoption of the name emotion-focused therapy (Greenberg's book of that year); the approach dates to the 1980s and 1990s as process-experiential therapy.
- **Process-experiential therapy** (medium; evidence: moderate): Status as a distinct therapy is debatable since the term is now largely superseded by emotion-focused therapy.
- **Family therapy (systemic family therapy)** (medium; evidence: strong): 'Family Systems' is ambiguous: it could mean Bowen family systems theory (covered under Bowenian) or be confused with Internal Family Systems (IFS); interpreted as systemic family therapy in general. Year 1956 (Bateson group's double-bind paper) is a conventional marker.
- **Structural family therapy** (medium; evidence: moderate): Year 1974 is the key book; the approach was developed through the 1960s. The extent to which family-based treatment for anorexia derives from structural therapy is a simplification.
- **Strategic family therapy** (medium; evidence: limited): Founding year uncertain; 1963 (Haley's book) is one marker, with Haley's Problem-Solving Therapy (1976) another. Evidence level reflects classic strategic therapy, not brief strategic family therapy.
- **Milan systemic therapy** (high; evidence: limited): Year could be given as 1971 (team formed) or 1975 (book); 1975 chosen.
- **Bowen family systems therapy** (medium; evidence: limited): Year 1966 (Bowen's paper on family theory in clinical practice) is approximate.
- **Emotionally focused couple therapy (EFT)** (medium; evidence: moderate): Exact first outcome study year (1985, Johnson and Greenberg) recalled rather than freshly verified.
- **Imago relationship therapy** (medium; evidence: limited): Founding year c.1980 is approximate. Results of the completed University of Pennsylvania Imago trial (NCT04302844) could not be found.
- **Solution-focused family therapy** (medium; evidence: moderate): Evidence level could reasonably be rated limited rather than moderate given variable trial quality. Founding year approximate.
- **Hakomi** (high; evidence: limited): Exact start year of Hakomi uncertain (mid-1970s); 1976 used as approximate.
- **Rolfing (Structural Integration)** (high; evidence: limited): Exact founding year is approximate (method developed gradually from c.1950s).
- **Dance movement psychotherapy** (medium; evidence: moderate): Verified that ADMP UK runs its own register and is an organisational member of UKCP (PSA-accredited); I could not confirm that ADMP's own register is itself PSA-accredited, so I did not say so. UK association founding year (1982) and Chace start date (c.1942) from memory. NICE CG178 'consider arts therapies' wording confirmed in secondary sources only.
- **Body psychotherapy** (medium; evidence: limited): NICE CG178 grouping of body psychotherapy within 'arts therapies' is from secondary sources describing the guideline; I am fairly but not fully certain it is in the current published wording.
- **Authentic Movement** (high; evidence: limited): Family assigned by me (so); could arguably be cr.
- **Art therapy (art psychotherapy)** (high; evidence: moderate): NICE CG178 recommendation wording ('consider offering arts therapies... particularly for negative symptoms') confirmed via secondary sources; MATISSE trial result from memory.
- **Music therapy** (high; evidence: moderate): Juliette Alvin society founding year (1958) from memory.
- **Dramatherapy** (high; evidence: limited): Slade 1939 and BADth 1977 dates from memory; year set at approximate start of the UK profession (1960s).
- **Poetry therapy** (medium; evidence: limited): Founders and dates (Greifer, Leedy; NAPT 1981) from memory, not web-verified. Lapidus reference from memory.
- **Sandplay therapy** (high; evidence: limited): Year 1956 approximate for Kalff's development of sandplay.
- **Bibliotherapy** (high; evidence: strong): evidenceLevel 'strong' applies to CBT-based guided self-help bibliotherapy only; creative bibliotherapy has limited evidence.
- **PhotoTherapy and therapeutic photography** (medium; evidence: limited): Name read as photo-based counselling (Weiser), not light therapy. Founding year approximate (mid-1970s); not web-verified.
- **Transcendental Meditation-based approaches** (medium; evidence: moderate): PTSD trial (Nidich et al. 2018, veterans, non-inferior to prolonged exposure) and 'TM international from 1958' from memory. evidenceLevel 'moderate' is a judgement given conflict-of-interest concerns; could be 'limited'.
- **Mindfulness-based stress reduction (MBSR)** (high; evidence: moderate): evidenceLevel 'strong' rests mainly on MBCT's NICE recommendation (NG222) for relapse prevention; MBSR itself is closer to moderate. Editor: evidence label set to moderate for MBSR itself; NICE’s recommendation is for its relative MBCT.
- **Vipassana-based meditation approaches** (medium; evidence: limited): Goenka start date (1969) and prison/substance-use study findings from memory, not web-verified. Year null because the practice is traditional.
- **Zen-based therapy** (medium; evidence: limited): Name is ambiguous: read as Zen-informed psychotherapy, especially David Brazier's Zen Therapy (1995 book); it could instead refer to Morita therapy or general Zen-influenced practice. Brazier and Morita dates from memory.
- **Animal-assisted play therapy** (medium; evidence: limited): Year 2008 is an approximation (VanFleet's book Play Therapy with Kids and Canines appeared in 2008; the AAPT model was formalised around then and in a later 2017 book). Could not confirm an exact founding date.
- **Therapeutic riding** (high; evidence: limited): Year 1952 marks Lis Hartel's influence rather than a formal founding; approximate.
- **Wilderness therapy** (medium; evidence: limited): Year 1970 is an approximation for the emergence of US wilderness therapy programmes. Chose evidenceLevel 'limited' rather than 'harmful' because harms are documented in specific programmes rather than in the approach as a whole; editors may wish to review.
- **Adventure therapy** (high; evidence: limited): Year chosen as 1971 (Project Adventure); Outward Bound dates from 1941 but was educational rather than therapeutic.
- **Brief strategic therapy** (medium; evidence: limited): Name is ambiguous: interpreted as the MRI/Nardone brief strategic therapy; could alternatively mean Szapocznik's Brief Strategic Family Therapy (BSFT, Miami, 1970s), which has a stronger trial base.
- **Ericksonian hypnotherapy** (high; evidence: limited): Year 1957 (founding of ASCH) is a marker; Erickson's approach developed gradually from the 1930s.
- **Regression therapy** (medium; evidence: none): Year 1970 is approximate (popularisation of past-life regression); age regression in hypnosis is older. evidenceLevel set to 'none' although documented harms from recovered-memory work could justify 'harmful'; editors may wish to review.
- **Parts therapy (hypnotherapy)** (medium; evidence: limited): Source named 'Roy Hunt'; this appears to be Roy Hunter, student of Charles Tebbetts. Year 1970 is an approximation; no primary source found for when Tebbetts first developed the technique.
- **Integrative therapy** (medium; evidence: moderate): evidenceLevel 'moderate' reflects trial evidence for specific integrative models; generic integrative counselling might be rated 'limited'.
- **Peer support** (medium; evidence: limited): Year 1970 is an approximation for a practice with diverse origins. Did not state NICE status: NICE psychosis guidance (CG178) is believed to suggest considering peer support, but this was not verified.
- **Virtual reality (VR) therapy** (medium; evidence: moderate): NICE early value assessment (HTE15/HTG701) of gameChange: recommends use with evidence generation for people aged 16 and over; exact current wording and status not fully verified.
- **AI-assisted therapy** (medium; evidence: emerging): Fast-moving area as of late 2026; details of regulation (e.g. US state laws such as Illinois's 2025 ban on AI therapy, Woebot's app closure in 2025) and UK NHS deployments (e.g. Limbic, Wysa) and their medical device status were not fully verified and may change.
- **Neurofeedback** (medium; evidence: limited): Statement that neurofeedback is not recommended in NICE ADHD guidance (NG87) is based on its absence from the recommendations; not verified against exact wording.
- **MDMA-assisted therapy** (medium; evidence: emerging): 2026 resubmission by the renamed company (Resilient, formerly Lykos) is from news/MAPS reports; FDA acceptance or outcome unknown as of November 2026. Australia has allowed authorised psychiatrists to prescribe MDMA for PTSD since July 2023 (not mentioned in text). Origin year c.1977 is approximate.
- **Psilocybin-assisted therapy** (medium; evidence: emerging): Compass Pathways completed its FDA application for COMP360 in November 2026 per news reports; approval status unknown. UK government agreed in principle in 2025 to ease research licensing for Schedule 1 drugs; psilocybin itself still Schedule 1. Year 1960 approximate (early psilocybin research c. late 1950s-1960s).
- **Ketamine-assisted psychotherapy (KAP)** (medium; evidence: emerging): Origin attribution (Salvador Roquet, Mexico, 1970s; Krupitsky, Russia) and year 1973 are approximate. NICE esketamine decision (final appraisal 2022, not recommended) - not verified whether revisited since. ACMD advised in January 2026 against moving ketamine to Class A; government decision not confirmed.
- **Psychedelic integration therapy** (medium; evidence: limited): No single founder or founding date; year left null. Evidence statement reflects absence of known trials rather than a systematic review.
- **Chakra balancing** (high; evidence: none): Ancient origin, so year left null; date of the modern Western form is approximate.
- **Crystal healing** (high; evidence: none): Ancient roots; year null. The placebo-crystal experiment (Christopher French and colleagues, 2001) was a conference presentation, so not named.
- **Shamanic healing** (medium; evidence: limited): Evidence level 'limited' rather than 'none' because a few small studies exist; could reasonably be 'none'.
- **Curanderismo** (medium; evidence: limited): Traditional practice, year null. Scope of UK availability not verified.
- **Native American healing traditions** (medium; evidence: limited): The 2009 Arizona sweat lodge deaths were at a self-help retreat run by a non-Native leader. Legal protection reference is to the US American Indian Religious Freedom Act 1978; Canadian changes differ in timing.
- **Primal therapy** (high; evidence: none): Status 'retired' reflects mainstream disuse, although a few practitioners continue.
- **Rebirthing** (high; evidence: harmful): Year 1974 is the approximate start of Orr's rebirthing breathwork.
- **Erhard Seminars Training (est)** (medium; evidence: limited): Ambiguous name. In a list beside primal scream, orgone and rebirthing, 'EST' most plausibly means Erhard Seminars Training; it could alternatively mean electroshock therapy (ECT), which is current, not retired. NICE ECT guidance (TA59 and NG222) stated from knowledge, not re-verified online.
- **Psychosurgery (lobotomy and leucotomy)** (high; evidence: harmful): Section 57 MHA 1983 stated from knowledge (requires consent plus certification by a second-opinion doctor and two others); not re-verified online. Deep brain stimulation is not destructive and is regulated differently.
- **Insulin shock therapy** (high; evidence: harmful): The 1950s controlled comparison is the Ackner and Oldham trial (1957); not named in text.
- **Psychiatric hydrotherapy (historical)** (medium; evidence: harmful): Year 1890 is a rough marker for widespread asylum use. 'Harmful' evidence level reflects documented injury and use as restraint rather than large-scale deaths; 'limited' would also be defensible.
- **Mesmerism** (high; evidence: none): Year 1775 approximate (Mesmer's first writings on animal magnetism c.1774-1779).
