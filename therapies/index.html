How to read this: the 0–1 scores are judgements assigned in the source essay, not measurements. This page maps how therapies differ in approach; it is not clinical advice. Choosing a therapy is a conversation with a GP or therapist. In England you can refer yourself to NHS Talking Therapies. If you need help now: NHS 111 (option 2), or Samaritans on 116 123.

Sources: the USF therapy landscape report (14 Aug 2025) and Tudor Watson, “The Therapeutic Landscape in 2025” (Aug 2025): six dimensions, 15 category profiles and the examples quoted. Where the essay gives only a category average, a therapy is placed at its category’s average, with any score the essay states for that therapy taking priority. Notes on UK guidance summarise NICE recommendations and may change; check the current guideline. Companion to Mathematics In Motion. Keys: ←/→ scenes, space play/pause, T try it.
