How to read this: positions on the map come from 0–1 judgements in the source essay and report, not measurements. Entries describe approaches; they are not clinical advice. Choosing a therapy is a conversation with a GP or therapist; in England you can refer yourself to NHS Talking Therapies. If you need help now: NHS 111 (option 2), or Samaritans on 116 123.

Sources: the USF therapy landscape report (14 Aug 2025) and Tudor Watson, “The Therapeutic Landscape in 2025”, for families, scores, distances and pathways. Entry text (what it is, origins, evidence, practice, suitability, UK access) was researched and written for this atlas from published sources; it summarises and may contain errors — check with a professional or the current NICE guideline.
