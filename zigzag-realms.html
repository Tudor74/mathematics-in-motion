Zigzag Realms

A river with three towns, a woodland and its magical grove, a mountain with lakes, a waterfall and caves, and a desert are laid out when your journey begins, with townsfolk and creatures living among them. Claude writes everything else one step ahead of you. Every place is kept as a cell in a ZigZag structure, so you can find your way back.

Who knows whom

● townsfolk● creatures● you— friendly— wary

    Ties grow when people and creatures spend time together, share food or face danger side by side, and fade when they are apart. Friends of friends drift closer. People and creatures move toward those they like and away from those they distrust.

    The garden

    Each flower is a group held together by friendly ties. More members means more petals; tighter circles of friends grow more layers; stronger ties grow longer petals; a blooming graph fills the seed heads. When ties sour the flowers droop, shed petals and grow thorns.

    Codex of hidden rules

      The realm script

      26 letters

        English, written in letters grown from their ancient pictographs: ox, house, door, hand, water, eye, mouth, head, tooth, mark. Each letter's shape, sound, ancestral meaning and frequency were reduced to three principal components that set its strokes, and a search kept frequent letters light and hard to confuse. Hover a letter for its origin. Switch the whole page to it with the Script button.
